Supply Chain Risk Assessment Report - CPG Sector

Executive Summary:
The CPG supply chain faces significant disruptions from three primary sources: raw material shortages,
logistics bottlenecks, and supplier concentration risk. Key findings indicate that 60% of tier-1
suppliers are concentrated in Southeast Asia, creating geopolitical exposure.

Key Risks:
1. Supplier Concentration: 15 of 25 critical ingredient suppliers are single-source.
2. Lead Times: Average lead time has increased from 45 days to 78 days over the past 12 months.
3. Inventory Buffers: Safety stock coverage has declined from 30 days to 18 days.
4. Logistics Costs: Ocean freight rates have increased 340% compared to 2022 baseline.

Recommendations:
- Dual-source critical ingredients within 6 months
- Increase safety stock for top 20 SKUs to 45 days coverage
- Negotiate long-term freight contracts to lock in rates
- Implement supplier financial health monitoring dashboard

Financial Impact:
Estimated annual exposure: $12.4M from supply disruptions
Cost of mitigation program: $2.1M
Net benefit of action: $10.3M annually
