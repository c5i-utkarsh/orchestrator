SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================

Nexus Global Operations (NGO) is a leading Fortune 500 consumer goods
manufacturer with 47 distribution centres across 18 countries, annual
procurement spend of £2.4Bn, and a global logistics network serving
142 markets. The Supply Chain Intelligence Programme ("the Programme")
is a strategic initiative to deploy an AI-powered supply chain
visibility, risk detection, and demand forecasting platform.

Programme Sponsor: James Whitfield, VP Global Supply Chain
Programme Director: Amara Osei, Head of Supply Chain Analytics
Delivery Partner: Axiom Analytics Partners (contracted for AI build)

Total approved budget: £4,800,000
Programme duration: 24 months (January 2024 – December 2025)
Target go-live: Q3 2024 (Phase 1)

================================================================
SECTION 2: BUSINESS CASE
================================================================

Current State:
- Supply chain disruptions in 2022-2023 cost NGO an estimated £34M in
  expediting charges, lost sales, and emergency procurement premiums.
- 73% of disruptions were detected reactively — after the supply impact
  had already materialised.
- Demand forecasting accuracy (MAPE) stands at 18.4% across top-50
  SKUs, generating excess safety stock of approximately £87M.
- 3PL performance is tracked manually via monthly spreadsheet reviews
  with a 3-4 week lag from actual delivery performance.

Target State:
- Real-time disruption detection for tariff changes, port closures,
  weather events, and supplier financial distress signals.
- MAPE improvement to below 12% across top-200 SKUs via demand-sensing.
- OTIF monitoring in near-real-time (T+24h) across all 3PL partners.
- Working capital reduction of £18M through optimised safety stock.

ROI Assumptions:
- 60% reduction in reactive disruption events: £20M saving
- Safety stock optimisation at 18% WC reduction: £15.6M benefit
- 3PL performance improvement via contract enforcement: £4.3M saving
- Total 3-year NPV at 12% WACC: £29.4M (after £4.8M programme cost)
