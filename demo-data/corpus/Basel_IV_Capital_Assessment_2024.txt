1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================

This assessment quantifies Meridian Bank Group's capital position
under the Basel IV / CRR III framework effective 1 January 2025,
identifies binding constraints arising from the Output Floor, and
sets out the management actions to maintain CET1 headroom above
the Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process (SREP) requirement.

Key findings:
  - Standardised Approach (SA) RWA: £14.8Bn (corporate portfolio)
  - Output Floor (72.5% of IRB RWA): Binding at £13.4Bn
  - CET1 capital requirement uplift: £176M (additional to current hold)
  - Post-floor CET1 ratio: 14.2% (vs 10.5% regulatory minimum + buffers)
  - Headroom above combined buffer requirement: 370bps

================================================================
2. OUTPUT FLOOR MECHANICS
================================================================

The Output Floor requires that RWAs calculated under the IRB approach
do not fall below 72.5% of the equivalent SA calculation.

  IRB RWA (corporate lending):       £11.2Bn
  SA RWA (corporate lending):        £14.8Bn
  Output Floor (72.5% × £14.8Bn):   £13.4Bn
  Binding constraint:                 Floor (not IRB)
  Incremental RWA vs pre-Basel IV:   +£2.2Bn
  CET1 impact (at 8% Pillar 1):      £176M additional capital required

The Group holds sufficient surplus capital to absorb this requirement
without a rights issue. Management action options (£200M capacity):
  a) AT1 issuance (planned Q4 2024, £150M tap): mitigates 85%
  b) RWA optimisation: secured lending substitution in corporate book
  c) Portfolio rebalancing: selective de-risking of high-risk SME book

================================================================
3. LARGE EXPOSURE LIMITS — SINGLE NAME BREACHES
================================================================

Per CRR Article 395, single-name exposures must not exceed 25% of Tier
1 capital (£2.9Bn for Meridian).

Current breaches (as at 31 March 2024):
  - Argent Properties plc: £3.1Bn exposure (107% of limit — BREACH)
    Route: unsecured revolving credit + bilateral TL + bond holding
    Action: 90-day remediation plan agreed with Risk Committee.
    Board notification: 12 April 2024.
    Remediation: mature £200M bilateral at July facility review; no
    new origination without CRO approval.

  - Nexus Financial: £3.0Bn (103% of limit — BREACH)
    Route: repo exposure + derivatives portfolio (netted)
    Action: Netting agreement renegotiation underway (Linklaters).
