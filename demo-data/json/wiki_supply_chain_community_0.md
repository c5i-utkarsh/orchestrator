## Key Passages

> A 25% tariff increase would add approximately £4.2M to Q3 procurement costs across 14 affected SKU families. Three qualified Vietnamese suppliers have confirmed capacity and can onboard within 6–8 weeks via existing supplier agreements.

> Master Logistics Agreement (MLA) 2023 defines OTIF target 95%. Performance credit: 1.5% monthly freight spend per percentage point below 95%. FastFreight UK at 88.3% — breach of -6.7pp, credit £87K/month.

> Identified 10 critical single-source dependencies: SemiCore (IC substrates, 23% production exposure), Nexus Plastics (injection-moulded housings, 19%), FlexSeal GmbH (industrial seals, 17%). Dual-source programmes recommended with 6–18 month qualification timelines.

> Demand forecast for FMCG-HPC-022: July 1.2M units, August 1.4M (+17% back-to-school), September 1.9M (+58% including 6-week promotional campaign). Recommend pre-building 800K units in June/July to buffer against September demand spike.

> Applying (s,S) replenishment policy with demand-sensing inputs across 12 SKUs yields a projected 21.4% working capital reduction. Safety stock recalculated using Poisson demand model at 99.2% fill rate.
