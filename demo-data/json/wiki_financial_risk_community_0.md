## Key Passages

> Standardised Approach RWA for corporate portfolio: £14.8Bn. Output Floor (72.5% of internal model RWA) binding constraint at £13.4Bn vs internal model £11.2Bn — floor adds £2.2Bn RWA uplift and £176M additional CET1 capital requirement.

> 23 transactions identified meeting TBML over-invoicing criteria. 8 high-priority alerts recommended for SAR filing — consistent invoice-to-market-price ratios exceeding 240% for commodity goods (electronics, textiles) via 4 corporate entities in UAE, Hong Kong and Cyprus.

> Under SBP scenario: retail mortgage PD increases from 1.2% to 4.7%; unsecured consumer from 3.8% to 9.1%; SME commercial from 2.4% to 7.3%. Stage 3 loan loss provisions increase by £1.84Bn. CET1 ratio falls from 14.2% to 11.8%.

> Retail Mortgage PD Model v7.2: Development Gini 0.73, Current Gini 0.61, degradation -16.4% (MRM Status: RED). Immediate redevelopment authorised. SME Scorecard v3.1: degradation -19.4% (MRM RED). External validation by PWC ordered.

> Large exposure breaches: Argent Properties plc £3.1Bn (107% of CRR limit), Nexus Financial £3.0Bn (103% of limit). 90-day remediation plans in place.
